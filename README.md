# 🧭 PathPilot

### Navigate your path. Discover your next move.

**PathPilot** is a modern platform designed to help users turn uncertainty into a clearer, more actionable path.

Instead of simply giving information, PathPilot focuses on helping users **understand where they are, explore where they can go, and identify what to do next.**

---

## ✨ What is PathPilot?

Choosing the right direction can be difficult when there are too many possibilities and not enough clarity.

PathPilot aims to make that process simpler through an interactive experience that brings **guidance, exploration, and decision-making** together in one place.

> **Your path isn't always obvious.
> PathPilot helps you find it.**

---
